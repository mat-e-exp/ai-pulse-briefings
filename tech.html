AI Pulse Engine

Automated Intelligence Pipeline

Overview

AI Pulse is a fully automated intelligence platform that continuously collects data from multiple sources, analyses content using advanced AI, and generates sentiment-based market opinions. The system is designed to be sector-agnostic - while currently focused on AI and technology markets, the underlying architecture can be applied to any data source, industry sector, or news feed. By removing manual intervention from the analysis pipeline, AI Pulse delivers consistent, unbiased intelligence briefings on a daily schedule.

Technical Architecture

The platform operates through a five-stage automated pipeline. Data is collected via API integrations from news services, market data providers, research publications, and social sentiment sources. A semantic deduplication engine filters redundant content using embedding-based similarity matching. Each unique event is then processed through Claude AI for sentiment analysis, significance scoring, and market impact classification. The system consolidates daily intelligence into a market direction prediction before market open, then validates accuracy against actual market close prices - creating a continuous feedback loop for performance measurement.

Roadmap

Future development focuses on three key areas: expanding data coverage through premium paid news sources and real-time feeds; enhancing analysis capabilities with multi-model LLM evaluation using the latest foundation models; and implementing ML-driven agents that continuously refine prediction algorithms, optimise source weighting, and evolve the system's analytical capabilities over time. The goal is a self-improving intelligence platform that becomes more accurate and comprehensive with each iteration.
